  • Turing FP16 for Forge Neo (opens in a new tab)NewModels that Forge Neo would run in float32 on GPUs without bfloat16 run in float16 instead.Forge & Stable Diffusion0

    Many models list only bfloat16 and float32, so an RTX 20, Titan RTX or GTX 16 falls back to float32 and runs several times slower. This changes that one fallback to float16 and leaves every other choice of Forge Neo untouched.

    Measured on an RTX 2070 with Forge Neo 2.31: MOSS-Image SFT at 1024×1024 went from 33 to 16 seconds a picture, the same picture down to small details. Other models have not been tested yet. A setting turns it off, since a model whose numbers outgrow float16 can come out black or burnt.

    Forge Neo · Performance
